### Exploratory testing: Calendar tab reselection

| ID | Test | Steps | Expected result | Actual result | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EX-01 | Tap the Calendar tab while already on the Calendar (before fix) | Scroll down the Calendar, then tap **Calendar** in the tab bar. | The calendar scrolls back to the top, as iPhone tab bars usually do. | The calendar did not scroll back to the top. | Observed | FAIL, found during exploratory testing |
| TC-36 | Retest after fix | Repeat EX-01 after Codex's fix. | The calendar scrolls back to the top. | The calendar scrolled back to the top. | Observed | PASS |

**Issue:** Tapping the already-selected Calendar tab did not scroll the calendar back to the top.
**Resolution:** Codex added `shouldScrollCalendarToTop` (`src/logic/navigation.ts`), which `App.tsx` uses to scroll the monthly calendar to the top when **Calendar** is tapped again. It only applies to the monthly calendar; when a Daily Schedule or profile is open, tapping **Calendar** returns to the calendar as before.
**Regression evidence:** TC-36 (manual retest) and AT-25 (automated). The scroll-to-top from a Daily Schedule or profile was not checked manually.
